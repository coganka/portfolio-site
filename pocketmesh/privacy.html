PocketMesh 3D · Privacy

Your models stay yours.

PocketMesh 3D works on your device. No account, advertising, tracking, or analytics SDK.

Effective date: 11 November 2026. This policy applies to PocketMesh 3D for iPhone and iPad, developed by Can Ogan Karagün.

Information the app handles

PocketMesh 3D stores your models, materials, project settings, animation, and recovery copies on your device. The app does not transmit these files, identifiers, or usage events to the developer or an app server. It has no account system, advertising SDK, tracking, or developer-operated analytics service.

Camera, scanning, and AR

Camera permission is optional and requested when you use object capture or AR. Modeling and procedural generation work without it. On supported devices, object scanning processes photos locally to make a textured model. Temporary capture photos and checkpoints are removed after processing or cancellation; files left by an interrupted session are cleared on a later app launch. The resulting mesh and compressed textures are saved only when you add them to a project. Camera images are not uploaded by PocketMesh 3D.

Files, sharing, and backups

You choose which files to import and where exported files go. A destination app, file provider, or cloud service handles the shared copy under its own policies. System device backups may include PocketMesh 3D’s local files according to your Apple backup settings. PocketMesh 3D does not operate a cloud sync service.

Retention and deletion

The current project and recovery copies stay on the device until removed. Deleting an object changes the current project but may leave older recovery copies. Removing the app deletes its local app data; copies you exported or backed up elsewhere must be removed separately. Undo history lasts only for the current session.

Support and this website

If you voluntarily email support, your email address, message, and any attachments are used to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive files. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve and follow up on the request; contact us to request deletion, subject to applicable record-keeping obligations. Email providers process correspondence under their own policies.

This static website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process request information such as your IP address for delivery and security under GitHub’s privacy statement. These PocketMesh 3D pages do not add analytics scripts or advertising cookies. Opening an external website is governed by that site’s policy.

Children and changes

PocketMesh 3D does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. If you believe a child has supplied personal information by email, contact us. Material changes to these practices will be reflected in this policy and its effective date.
