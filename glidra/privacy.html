Glidra · Privacy

Privacy policy

Your puzzle progress stays on your device. Ads and optional purchases use Google and Apple services.

Effective date: 12 November 2026. This policy applies to Glidra for iOS, developed by Can Ogan Karagün.

Local game data

Glidra stores level progress, records, preferences, and a cached Remove Ads entitlement locally. There is no player account or developer-operated gameplay server. Device backups may include this data according to your Apple settings. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to system backup behavior.

Advertising

Glidra integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for occasional interstitial advertising and consent choices. Google may process your IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and crash, diagnostic, and performance information to deliver and measure ads, detect fraud, and maintain its services. The app does not request access to your precise location.

Glidra requests non-personalized ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Non-personalized ads can still use data for contextual delivery, measurement, frequency limits, and fraud prevention. Required consent is requested before ads are initialized. Where applicable, you can revisit your privacy choices from Options. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Purchases

Remove Ads is an optional, non-consumable purchase processed by Apple through Unity In-App Purchasing. Apple handles payment and billing information; the developer does not receive your card details. The app uses purchase and entitlement information to grant and restore ad removal. The cached entitlement remains on your device; the game does not send receipts or transaction identifiers to a developer-operated server. Apple's processing is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Retention and your choices

Local progress and preferences remain until you remove the app or change them. Apple and Google retain service data according to their policies and applicable requirements. Use in-game privacy choices where available, your Apple privacy settings, and the providers' privacy controls to manage your choices. Purchasing Remove Ads stops interstitial ads in the game; it does not delete data already held by service providers. Contact us with privacy requests or questions.

Support and this website

If you email support, we use your email address, message, and attachments to respond. Correspondence is kept as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion by email. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

This static website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request data, including IP addresses, for delivery and security under its privacy statement. These Glidra pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts. External sites have their own policies.

Children

Glidra is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information. If you believe a child provided personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.
